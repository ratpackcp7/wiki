# DeepSeek-V3.2

On December 1, 2025, DeepSeek released two new open-weight flagship models. MIT licensed.

## Model Specifications
- **Models:** DeepSeek-V3.2 and DeepSeek-V3.2-Speciale
- **Size:** 685B parameters (690GB)
- **Availability:** Open weights on Hugging Face
- **License:** MIT

## Key Differences: V3.2 vs Speciale
- V3.2: General flagship — reasoning, agentic tasks, human alignment. Distilled from specialists.
- Speciale: Experimental — extended reasoning and mathematical proofs. Trained exclusively on reasoning data with reduced length penalty during RL. Scores gold on 2025 IMO.
