# The Resonant Computing Manifesto

**Source:** [Simon Willison's Weblog](https://simonwillison.net/2025/Dec/5/resonant-computing/)  
**Date:** December 5, 2025  
**Key Figures:** Simon Willison (Founding Signatory), Alex Komoroske (Lead Instigator), Steven Levy (Wired Journalist)

## Overview
Launched at WIRED's *The Big Interview* event, the **Resonant Computing Manifesto** proposes a positive framework for building hyper-personalized, AI-powered software. It aims to move away from the "attention hijacking" and "one-size-fits-all" patterns that dominated the previous decade of software design.

## The Core Philosophy: AI as a Solution to Standardization
The manifesto argues that traditional software scaling required "sanding away edge cases" to serve the average user. AI changes this economic and technological necessity by allowing software to adapt to individual context.

> "Software can now respond fluidly to the context and particularity of each human—at scale. One-size-fits-all is no longer a technological or economic necessity. Where once our digital environments inevitably shaped us against our will, we can now build technology that *adaptively shapes itself* in service of our individual and collective aspirations."

## The Five Principles of Resonant Software
The manifesto outlines five pillars for a new era of computing:

1. **Private:** Data remains under personal stewardship and is kept private.
2. **Dedicated:** Software is built to serve the user's specific interests rather than the platform's.
3. **Plural:** Distributed control and pluralism instead of platform monopolies.
4. **Adaptable:** Tools that mold themselves to individual human contexts.
5. **Prosocial:** Designed for healthy membership in shared digital and physical spaces.

## Context and Origins
* **Inspiration:** The movement draws parallels to the **Malleable Software** concept (Ink & Switch) and the architectural philosophy of Christopher Alexander.
* **The "Why":** Lead instigator Alex Komoroske notes that by 2025, Big Tech had strayed from its idealistic roots, aligning more with political and monopolistic interests.
* **Definition of "Resonant":** Chosen for its positive connotations, described by the document as: *"The experience of encountering something that speaks to our deeper values."*
