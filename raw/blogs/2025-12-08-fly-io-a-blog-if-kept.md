# A Blog, If You Can Keep It

Thomas Ptacek on Fly.io's blog strategy shift. Moving from carefully choreographed "EffortPosts" optimized for Hacker News to "normal blog like it's 2008."

Old formula: Dense technical pieces >2000 words, no promotion, HN-friendly topics, avoid certain "cat-repellants." Limited to ~1-2 posts/month to not "wear out welcome."

Realization: Simon Willison disproved the "wearing out welcome" theory by writing daily about LLMs without choreographing.

New approach: Write more frequently, stop gatekeeping, clear space for other team members. The editorial function serialized the team.

Self-deprecating: "I'm real dumb about a lot of things." But excited to write more freely.
