# Accident Forgiveness

Fly.io's Explicit Accident Forgiveness policy for cloud billing.

Problem: Elastic cloud billing creates financial anxiety. Miskeyed configs can lead to massive unexpected charges.

Industry reality: Major providers have billing teams that often concede disputes, but process is slow/uncertain.

Solution: Official, automated refund of unexpected charges for customers with support contracts. No hard limit on usage. Every forgiven bill triggers manual review for product improvements.

Challenge: Fraud (crypto miners exploit open-ended forgiveness).

Future: Moving from refunds to prevention — spike detection, backdated reservations, billing alerts.

Key quote: "Your messed-up CI/CD jobs didn't really cost us anything, so if you didn't really want them, they shouldn't cost you anything either."
