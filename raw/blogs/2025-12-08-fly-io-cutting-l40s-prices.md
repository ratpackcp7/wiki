# We're Cutting L40S Prices In Half

Fly.io NVIDIA L40S GPU price cut to $1.25/hr (matching A10 price).

Goal: Shift demand from over-subscribed A10s to superior L40S hardware.

L40S is AI-optimized L40 (data center RTX 4090). Retains rendering hardware unlike A100/H100. Best for: Llama 3.1 70B, Flux, Whisper, bioinformatics, cloud gaming.

Fly.io advantage: Tigris storage close to compute, Anycast networking, high RAM VMs, no egress traps.

Key quote: "If you're trying to do something GPU-accelerated in response to an HTTP request, the right combination of GPU, instance RAM, fast object storage, and networking is much more important than getting your hands on an H100."
