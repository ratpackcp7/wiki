# 2025 Word of the Year: Slop

**Source:** [Simon Willison's Weblog](https://simonwillison.net/2025/Dec/15/2025-word-of-the-year-slop/)  
**Date:** December 15, 2025  

15th December 2025 - Link Blog

**[2025 Word of the Year: Slop](https://www.merriam-webster.com/wordplay/word-of-the-year)**. Slop lost to "brain rot" for [Oxford Word of the Year 2024](https://simonwillison.net/2024/Nov/15/slop-word-of-the-year/) but it's finally made it this year thanks to Merriam-Webster!

> Merriam-Webster's human editors have chosen slop as the 2025 Word of the Year. We define slop as "digital content of low quality that is produced usually in quantity by means of artificial intelligence."
