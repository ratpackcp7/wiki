# Summary: How Google Got Its Groove Back and Edged Ahead of OpenAI

This article by Simon Willison analyzes Google's resurgence in the AI sector, drawing from a Wall Street Journal report and Alphabet's 2025 financial data. It highlights key personnel shifts, internal anecdotes, and concrete usage metrics that signal Google's competitive standing against OpenAI.

## Key Insights & Anecdotes

### The Return of Sergey Brin
A pivotal moment in Google's AI pivot was the "un-retirement" of co-founder Sergey Brin, reportedly spurred by a conversation with an OpenAI researcher.

> "Around that time, Google co-founder Sergey Brin, who had recently retired, was at a party chatting with a researcher from OpenAI named Daniel Selsam... Why, Selsam asked him, wasn't he working full time on AI. Hadn't the launch of ChatGPT captured his imagination as a computer scientist? ... Brin decided Selsam had a point and returned to work."

### The "Nano Banana" Origin
The name for Google's internal tool "Nano Banana" was created by Naina Raisinghani at 2:30 a.m. as a mashup of two nicknames given to her by friends.

## Critical Usage Metrics (as of late 2025/early 2026)

The post provides rare, concrete figures regarding Gemini's growth and scale:

*   **Monthly Active Users (MAU):** Gemini reached **650 million** monthly users by October 2025, up from 450 million in July.
*   **Token Throughput:** Gemini models process **7 billion tokens per minute** via direct API use.
*   **Financial Milestone:** Alphabet reported its first-ever **$100 billion quarter** in Q3 2025.

### Comparison to OpenAI
While Gemini reports **650 million monthly** users, OpenAI reported **800 million weekly** active users for ChatGPT (as of October 2025). 

> **Note on Data:** Willison clarifies that the "Gemini App" figure likely includes the Android/iOS apps and direct visits to `gemini.google.com`, rather than passive interactions within Gmail or Google Docs.

## Summary of Alphabet Q3 2025 Earnings Report
Sundar Pichai confirmed the following in the official earnings statement:
*   Double-digit growth across all major business segments.
*   Gemini models are consistently "topping leaderboards."
*   The **650 million MAU** figure specifically refers to the "Gemini App."
