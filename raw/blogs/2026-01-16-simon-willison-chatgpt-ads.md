# Our approach to advertising and expanding access to ChatGPT

## 1. Advertising Rollout
OpenAI has officially announced the testing of advertisements within ChatGPT to support broader access and reduced usage limits for non-paying users.

*   **Timeline:** Testing begins in the "coming weeks" (early 2026) in the U.S.
*   **Affected Tiers:** Ads will appear in the **Free** and **Go** tiers.
*   **Ad-Free Tiers:** Pro, Business, and Enterprise subscriptions remain ad-free.

### Core Commitments on Ad Integration
OpenAI has established two primary "guardrails" to address user concerns regarding AI integrity:
> * **Answer independence**: Ads do not influence the answers ChatGPT gives you. Answers are optimized based on what's most helpful to you. Ads are always separate and clearly labeled.
> * **Conversation privacy**: We keep your conversations with ChatGPT private from advertisers, and we never sell your data to advertisers.

## 2. The New "ChatGPT Go" Tier
OpenAI has introduced a new mid-range subscription tier called **ChatGPT Go**.

*   **Price:** $8/month in the USA.
*   **History:** Previously trialed in India (August 2025) for ₹399/month (~$4.40).
*   **Positioning:** Designed for users who want more than the free tier but do not require the full $20/month Plus subscription.

## 3. Plan Comparison & Technical Limits
There is some ambiguity regarding specific usage limits for GPT-5.2, but the context windows are tiered as follows:

| Plan | Context Window |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Free** | 16K tokens |
| **Go** | 32K tokens |
| **Plus** | 32K tokens |
| **Pro** | 128K tokens |

*Note: While the GPT-5.2 model page lists a 400,000 token window, these limits are restricted within the ChatGPT interface.*

## 4. Ad Format and User Experience
Based on early screenshots, ads appear to be contextually relevant to the conversation but visually distinct.

*   **Example:** A query about Santa Fe, New Mexico, triggers a "Sponsored" section for a local business (e.g., "Pueblo & Pine" cottages).
*   **Interactive Ads:** The interface includes a **"Chat with [Advertiser]"** button.
*   **Insight:** Clicking this button transitions the user into a separate chat interface controlled by the advertiser's bot, where the advertiser *can* influence the responses.

## 5. Key Takeaways
*   **Monetization Shift:** OpenAI is moving toward a hybrid model (Subscription + Ads) to subsidize the high cost of running GPT-5.2 for free users.
*   **Tier Fragmentation:** The gap between "Free" (16K) and "Pro" (128K) context windows is significant, with the new "Go" tier offering a modest middle ground.
*   **Privacy vs. Utility:** While OpenAI claims conversations are private from advertisers, the "Chat with Advertiser" feature creates a direct pipeline for brands to engage with users based on their initial queries.
