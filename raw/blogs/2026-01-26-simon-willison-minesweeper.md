# Tool: Minesweeper

Tool [Minesweeper](https://tools.simonwillison.net/minesweeper) — Play a classic Minesweeper game directly in your browser with support for three difficulty levels: Easy, Medium, and Hard. Use left-click to reveal cells and right-click to place flags, or toggle between reveal and flag modes on mobile with long-press support.
