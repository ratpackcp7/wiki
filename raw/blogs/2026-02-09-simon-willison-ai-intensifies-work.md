# AI Doesn't Reduce Work—It Intensifies It

**Source:** Simon Willison's Weblog
**Date:** February 9, 2026
**Key Reference:** Harvard Business Review (HBR) study by Aruna Ranganathan and Xingqi Maggie Ye (Berkeley Haas School of Business).

---

## Core Insight: The "Exhausting" Productivity Boost
While AI is often marketed as a tool to reduce workload, initial findings from a 2025 study of 200 employees at a U.S. tech company suggest it actually **intensifies** work through increased cognitive load and parallel tasking.

### The New Work Rhythm
The study identifies a shift in how work is performed, moving from sequential tasks to a "juggling" model:
> "AI introduced a new rhythm in which workers managed several active threads at once: manually writing code while AI generated an alternative version, running multiple agents in parallel, or reviving long-deferred tasks because AI could "handle them" in the background."

### Key Effects on Workers
*   **Constant Context Switching:** Workers feel compelled to check AI outputs frequently while maintaining their own manual work.
*   **The "Partner" Illusion:** Having an AI "partner" creates a sense of momentum that encourages taking on more tasks than can be sustainably managed.
*   **Rapid Energy Depletion:** Simon Willison notes that while he can accomplish significantly more, his mental energy is often "entirely depleted" after only an hour or two.
*   **Addictive Feedback Loops:** The ease of "just one more prompt" leads to workers losing sleep and over-extending themselves because progress feels "irresistible."

---

## Organizational Challenges
The intensification of work creates a "fog" for management, making it difficult to distinguish between **genuine productivity gains** and **unsustainable intensity** that leads to burnout.

### Recommendations for Organizations
*   **Develop an "AI Practice":** Companies must create structured frameworks for AI usage rather than leaving it to individual discretion.
*   **Redefine Sustainability:** Acknowledge that decades of intuition regarding "a fair day's work" have been disrupted.
*   **Focus on Discipline:** Finding a new balance requires intentional discipline to prevent the "always-on" nature of AI from overwhelming human capacity.
