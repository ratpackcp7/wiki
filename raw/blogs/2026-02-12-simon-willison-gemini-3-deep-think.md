# Gemini 3 Deep Think

Google has released Gemini 3 Deep Think, a model designed to advance the "frontier of intelligence" for complex tasks in science, research, and engineering.

Key Capabilities:
- Reasoning & Logic: Positioned as a high-reasoning model (indicated by the "Deep Think" branding).
- SVG Generation: Demonstrates significant improvement in spatial reasoning and code-based image generation (SVG).
- Self-Description: The model is capable of generating highly detailed alt-text/descriptions of the images it creates.

Simon Willison uses a "Pelican riding a bicycle" prompt as a benchmark for LLM spatial reasoning and SVG coding ability. Gemini 3 Deep Think produced what Willison calls "the best one I've seen so far."

Basic Prompt Results (Generate an SVG of a pelican riding a bicycle):
- A "really good" flat vector illustration featuring a stylized pelican with a magenta cap, a scarf, and a basket with a fish.
- The model successfully handled complex layering and motion indicators (speed lines).

Advanced Challenge Prompt results:
- Mechanical Accuracy: The model rendered a "classic diamond frame," detailed silver spokes, a front chainring, a chain, and a rear cog.
- Biological Accuracy: It included the "vividly bright red, inflated throat pouch" and "breeding plumage" (white head, yellow crest, dark brown neck stripe).
- Artistic Interpretation: The model depicted the bird in an "aerodynamic racing posture."
