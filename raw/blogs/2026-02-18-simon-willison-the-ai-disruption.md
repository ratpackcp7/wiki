# The A.I. Disruption We've Been Waiting for Has Arrived

Simon Willison highlights Paul Ford's NYT piece on the massive shift in software economics and productivity following the "November 2025 inflection point" in AI coding capabilities.

Ford observation: In November 2025, Claude Code transitioned from "halting and clumsy" to highly capable agents that can run for a full hour and build whole websites and apps.

Cost comparison (2021 retail vs modern AI):
- Personal website reboot: ~$25,000 → included in subscription
- Complex data migration: ~$350,000 (PM, Designer, 2 Engineers, 4-6 months) → ~$200/mo Claude plan, done over a weekend

Ford: "All of the people I love hate this stuff, and all the people I hate love it. And yet, likely because of the same personality flaws that drew me to technology in the first place, I am annoyingly excited."
