# Tool: Animated Word Cloud

View animated word cloud visualizations that use an Archimedean spiral placement algorithm to position words on a canvas. Each word spirals outward from the center until it finds an unoccupied space, with grid-based spatial indexing accelerating collision detection to handle large datasets efficiently. Control animation playback speed, step through frames manually, download the final result as a PNG, or paste your own text to generate a custom word cloud.

Live tool: https://tools.simonwillison.net/animated-word-cloud.
