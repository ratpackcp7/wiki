# Summary: Greenfield and Iterative Development

Prime Radiant has released research previews for two new agentic development technologies: **Greenfield** and **Iterative Development**. These tools are designed to bridge the gap between legacy "brownfield" codebases and modern, agent-driven software reconstruction.

## 1. Key Technologies & Repositories

*   **[Greenfield](https://github.com/prime-radiant-inc/greenfield):** A suite for reverse-engineering existing software into behavioral specifications.
*   **[Iterative Development](https://github.com/prime-radiant-inc/iterative-development):** An agentic methodology for building large-scale products from detailed specs without losing requirements.
*   **[Superpowers](https://github.com/obra/superpowers):** The foundational agentic development methodology (created by Jesse Vincent) that Iterative Development augments.
