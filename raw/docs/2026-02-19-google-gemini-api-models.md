## Model Versioning & Lifecycle

### Version Types
- **Stable:** Specific versions that do not change. Recommended for production.
- **Preview:** Production-ready but subject to billing and 2-week deprecation notices.
- **Latest:** An alias pointing to the most recent release. Hot-swapped with 2-week notice via email.
- **Experimental:** Not for production; used for rapid feedback and testing.

### Deprecations
Models like Gemini 2.0 Flash and Gemini 2.0 Flash-Lite are now marked as Deprecated.
