# GGML and llama.cpp Join Hugging Face (HF)

**Published:** February 20, 2026
**Source:** [Hugging Face Blog](https://huggingface.co/blog/ggml-joins-hf)

## Overview
Hugging Face has officially announced that **GGML**, the creators of **llama.cpp**, are joining the company. This partnership aims to scale and support the community behind local AI inference, ensuring its long-term progress and open-source availability.

> "llama.cpp is the fundamental building block for local inference, and transformers is the fundamental building block for model definition, so this is basically a match made in heaven."

## Key Changes & Continuity
Despite the acquisition, the core operations of the projects will remain largely unchanged:
- **Autonomy:** Georgi Gerganov and his team will continue to dedicate 100% of their time to maintaining `llama.cpp` with full technical leadership and autonomy.
- **Open Source:** The project will remain **100% open-source** and community-driven.
- **Resources:** Hugging Face will provide long-term sustainable resources to help the project grow and thrive.

## Technical Focus & Roadmap
1. **Seamless Integration:** Working toward a "single-click" experience to ship new models from `transformers` directly into `llama.cpp`.
2. **User Experience (UX):** Improving packaging and deployment of GGML-based software. Simplifying access for "casual users."
3. **Ubiquity:** Making `llama.cpp` "ubiquitous and readily available everywhere."

## Long-Term Vision
> "Our shared goal is to provide the community with the building blocks to make open-source superintelligence accessible to the world over the coming years."

## Community Insights
- **Strategic Coherence:** Aligning Transformers (definition) with llama.cpp (inference) provides stable foundation for local AI.
- **Format Adoption:** GGUF becoming preferred default for other platforms (e.g., executorch for on-device inference).
- **Skepticism:** Concerns about future monetization/telemetry, though HF emphasizes open-source commitment.
- **Feature Requests:** Native image model quantization, agent harness integration.
